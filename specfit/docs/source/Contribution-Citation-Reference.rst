Contribution
============
If you want to contribute to the development of SpecFit you are warmyl welcomed.
Your contribution would be appreciated for several tasks:

* expanding support to further file Formats
* improving visualisation
* stabelising user-defined lines
* changing to DASK to allow import and evaluation of large datasets

alongside testing, bug reporting and fixing. Your ideas from your personal XRF
experience to further improve SpecFit are very welcomed. Contribution to the 
Documentation is always appreciated.

.. _ref-citation

Citation
========
If you used SpecFit for your XRF data evaluation please cite the following paper

`Förste, F. et al. - Quantification routines for full 3D elemental distributions of homogeneous and layered samples obtained with laboratory confocal micro XRF spectrometers <https://doi.org/10.1039/d2ja00119e>>`_
